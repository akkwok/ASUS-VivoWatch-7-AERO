Smart Sensing from Side to Back

Side Sensor

Effortless Instant Fingertip Insights

Lightly place your fingertip on the side sensor modules to instantly track on-demand Blood Pressure*, ECG*, Heart Rate and SpO2 in seconds. The high density of microvascular capillaries in your fingertips delivers stronger signals with less motion interference, providing quick readings that reflect your current vitality.

Back Sensor

Continuous Monitoring with Premium PVD Coating

Non-stop wellness guard. The rear sensor continuously tracks your vital signs day and night. Upgraded with an advanced vacuum-electroplated (PVD) coating, it resists sweat and oils, stays gentle on the skin, and wipes clean effortlessly.

Check Blood Pressure with ASUS VivoWatch 7 AERO

Taking an ECG with ASUS VivoWatch 7 AERO

Place your thumb and forefinger on the dual sensors of VivoWatch for 30 seconds to get detailed recordings on the electrocardiogram (ECG) app. Use ASUS HealthConnect to share ECG results with your healthcare provider.*

*ECG availability may vary by region.
